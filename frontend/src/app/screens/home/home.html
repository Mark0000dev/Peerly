How it works

Three steps, no awkward cold messages

1

Set a goal

Pick a subject and, if you like, the topic you’re stuck on. One active goal keeps things focused.

2

Swipe through people

See classmates who can help with your subject, need help with yours, or are working on the same thing.

3

Send a short request

Review it first; nothing is sent until you tap Send. A chat opens only when they accept.

Three ways to use it

Learn, help, or both

Find help

Meet someone who listed your subject as something they can help with.

Offer help

See who’s working on a subject you know well, and offer a hand.

Study together

Pair up with someone on the same goal. Nobody has to be the teacher.

Safety

Built for students, with people watching out

  • APU students only. Everyone confirms a student address. We only send it a code; we never read your mailbox.
  • Requests before chats. Nobody can message you until you accept.
  • Report and block. Ask the team to review a concern.
  • Take a break. Hide your profile and keep your chats.

Questions

Good to know

Who can join?

APU students with a Google account and an address ending in @mail.apu.edu.my.

Do you read my APU email?

No. We send a code to confirm the address. We never sign in to or read your mailbox.

Is there AI in it?

Optional assistance with subjects, wording and photos. You decide what suggestions to use.
