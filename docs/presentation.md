# Demo & Presentation

## Final demo scenario

1. A student opens StudyMate AI.
2. Creates a profile with subjects, skills, and availability.
3. Receives recommended study partners.
4. Views compatibility scores and AI explanations.
5. Selects two partners.
6. Creates a Study Party.
7. Creates and completes a study session.
8. Rates teammates.
9. Views updated study progress and streak.

This shows the whole product, from onboarding to collaborative learning and progress tracking.
